## Business recommendations
1. Review monthly trends to plan inventory and campaigns during weaker periods.
2. Protect availability of leading categories and products.
3. Compare city-level order volume and order value before allocating marketing spend.
4. Segment repeat and one-time customers and test retention campaigns.
5. Do not judge discount success using revenue alone; product cost and margin data are needed to assess profitability.
